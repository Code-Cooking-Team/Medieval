# Medieval

This glossary describes people, their roles and items, and the landscape of the game world.

## Language

**Profession**  
A person's role, such as Guardian or Woodcutter.

**Held Item**  
An object a person is assigned to hold, such as a sword or hatchet.

**Equipment Slot**  
A place on a person for one equipped item.

**Hands**  
The shared equipment slot for items held in either or both hands.

**Armor**  
The equipment slot for armor worn by a person.

**Ground Elevation**:
The height of the ground at a location relative to the map's vertical datum.

**Surface Type**:
The character of the ground surface at a location, such as meadow, sand, or rock.

## Relationships

- A person has two **Equipment Slots**, **Hands** and **Armor**.
- A **Held Item** occupies **Hands**.
- A **Profession** can provide a default **Held Item**.
- **Ground Elevation** defines the shape of the landscape independently of **Surface Type**. **Surface Type** can add small local variations.

## Example dialogue

> **Dev:** “What does a person receive when they become a Woodcutter?”  
> **Domain expert:** “Their default Held Item is a hatchet. The hatchet and the Profession are separate.”

> **Dev:** “If a rocky hillside becomes a meadow, does the hillside disappear?”\
> **Domain expert:** “It keeps its shape. The Surface Type changes the cover and small details of the ground.”
