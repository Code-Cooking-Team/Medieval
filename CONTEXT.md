# Medieval

This glossary describes people and the roles and items they have in the game.

## Language

**Profession**  
A person's role, such as Guardian or Woodcutter.

**Held Item**  
An object a person is assigned to hold, such as a sword or hatchet.

**Equipment Slot**  
A place on a person for one equipped item.

**Hands**  
The shared equipment slot for items held in either or both hands.

**Armor**  
The equipment slot for armor worn by a person.

## Relationships

- A person has two **Equipment Slots**, **Hands** and **Armor**.
- A **Held Item** occupies **Hands**.
- A **Profession** can provide a default **Held Item**.

## Example dialogue

> **Dev:** “What does a person receive when they become a Woodcutter?”  
> **Domain expert:** “Their default Held Item is a hatchet. The hatchet and the Profession are separate.”
