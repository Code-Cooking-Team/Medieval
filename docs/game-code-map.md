# Game code map

## Start with the symptom

| Change | Start here | Follow it to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Startup, reset, save/load | [App.tsx](../src/App.tsx) | [Game.ts](../src/game/Game.ts) owns the simulation, actor list, and snapshot. |
| Selection or orders | [SelectInteractions.ts](../src/game/player/interaction/methods/SelectInteractions.ts) | [HumanPlayer.ts](../src/game/player/HumanPlayer.ts) owns selection. Right-click tries the target actor's `interact` before assigning walkable destinations with flood fill. |
| Building placement | [BuildInteractions.ts](../src/game/player/interaction/methods/BuildInteractions.ts) | Build and Spawn share previews, raycasting, and validation of the rotated walls and interior. Paths can share space and leave existing obstacles intact. Build queues Construction Sites. Spawn places finished buildings immediately. |
| Movement or walkability | [WalkableActor.ts](../src/game/core/WalkableActor.ts) | [Pathfinding.ts](../src/game/core/Pathfinding.ts) uses the same step-height limit for EasyStar routes, multi-goal routes, and live movement. [World.ts](../src/game/world/World.ts) emits `tailUpdate` (actual spelling) on tile replacement so the running game can refresh pathfinding. |
| Unit jobs | [HumanActor.ts](../src/game/actors/units/human/HumanActor.ts) | A building interaction or confirmed construction placement assigns the profession. [BuilderProfession.ts](../src/game/professions/BuilderProfession.ts) owns the Build Queue and hauled wood. For woodcutting, trace [WoodCampActor.ts](../src/game/actors/buildings/woodCamp/WoodCampActor.ts) → [WoodcutterProfession.ts](../src/game/professions/WoodcutterProfession.ts) → [woodcutterMachine.ts](../src/game/professions/machines/woodcutterMachine.ts). |
| Hunting and meat | [HunterHutActor.ts](../src/game/actors/buildings/hunterHut/HunterHutActor.ts) → [HunterProfession.ts](../src/game/professions/HunterProfession.ts) | Right-click assigns owned humans and equips a spear. Hunters chase deer, retaliate against attackers, collect meat only on a killing blow, and deliver it to an owned hut. Hut stock and carried meat persist; `meatStockChanged` updates the HUD. |
| Terrain generation and water | [generateWorld.ts](../src/game/world/generateWorld.ts) | [drainage.ts](../src/game/world/drainage.ts) traces a drainage network across the heightfield. [generateRiverNetwork.ts](../src/game/world/generateRiverNetwork.ts) accumulates runoff, fits channel levels to nearby low ground, and blends low banks into the surrounding terrain. It also forms shallow backwaters along lowland reaches. [generateLakes.ts](../src/game/world/generateLakes.ts) carves separate lake basins in sheltered terrain away from the river network. [WaterRenderer.ts](../src/game/renderer/WaterRenderer.ts) meshes the shared water surface and uses waterfall shading only for drops marked inside the channel. |
| Camera, terrain, or mesh | [Renderer.ts](../src/game/Renderer.ts) | The renderer drives frames independently of the game tick. Look in [RTSCamera.ts](../src/game/core/RTSCamera.ts) for camera behavior, [renderer/](../src/game/renderer/) for terrain and effects, and the actor's folder for its mesh and animation. |
| Sidebar or settings | [ui/modules/](../src/ui/modules/) | [config.ts](../src/config/config.ts) defines settings and defaults. [createConfig.ts](../src/config/lib/createConfig.ts) handles persistence under the browser's `config` key. |

## Cross-layer changes

- **Actors:** Start with a sibling in [actors/](../src/game/actors/). An actor holds simulation state; a building blueprint supplies the model and footprint; a renderer handles visuals. Check `ActorType` in [types.ts](../src/game/types.ts) and the class, blueprint, and renderer registrations in [actors/index.ts](../src/game/actors/index.ts). Trees use the basic renderer list. Check [SpawnList.tsx](../src/ui/modules/spawn/SpawnList.tsx) when exposing an actor in the sidebar.
- **Coordinates:** Map and world positions are `[x, y]`; the grid is indexed `tiles[y][x]`. [updateObjectPosition](../src/helpers/three.ts) maps the ground plane to Three.js X/Z and height to Three.js Y, scaled by `config.renderer.tileSize`. When changing picking, compare [RaycastFinder.ts](../src/game/player/interaction/methods/lib/RaycastFinder.ts) with the forward transform, including the map-center offset.
- **Maps:** Fresh games generate 448 × 416 tiles with a random seed while `WORLD_TEST_SCALE` is set to `2`. Open `/Medieval/?seed=18` to replay a specific world. `generateWorld(seed, chunkY)` can generate a separate chunk to the north or south. Chunks share their outer row, including river crossing positions, flow directions, widths, and water levels. [riverRouting.ts](../src/game/world/riverRouting.ts) defines the crossings shared by adjacent chunks. [terrainRegions.ts](../src/game/world/terrainRegions.ts) sets the elevation of broad regions in world coordinates and varies the width of their boundaries to create cliff faces and walkable approaches. Each seed varies mountain coverage from open lowlands to mountains across the map, and independently varies how many tributaries join the main river. Layered noise shapes mountain ranges, banks, and surface colors.
- **Saves:** `App.tsx` stores `GameJSON` under `saved-game2`. `Game.fromJSON` restores the world and players, then resolves actor ownership by player ID and looks up actor classes by type. World saves include tile elevations, lake footprints and levels, and water surface samples for the drainage network. Older river ribbons remain readable. Saved channel samples mark where the flow descends through a waterfall. Older saves without water regions derive patches from their water tiles. When changing persisted state, check the relevant `toJSON` and `fromJSON` together and try a save/load round trip.
